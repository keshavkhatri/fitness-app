What to write to us about

  • Early access. StokeForm is Android first. Tell us you would like to try it.
  • Support. Something not working? Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, and your phone model and Android version.
  • Delete your account. Write from the email address on the account so that we can confirm it is you. We delete the account within 30 days.
  • Privacy requests and questions. Access, correction or deletion of your information, or anything in our Privacy Policy.
  • Anything else. Feedback, ideas and press questions are welcome.

Please do not email passwords, or health records or reports, unless we have asked for them. Your fitness data lives on your phone, and we do not need it to help you.
