Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the StokeForm app (the "app") and this website (stokeform.com) collect, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. "StokeForm", "we" and "us" mean the people who run the app. If you have a question, write to support@stokeform.com.

In short: we ask for an email address and a name to create your account. Your workouts, food, body measurements and health details are stored on your phone, encrypted. They are copied to our server only if you turn on cloud backup, which is off until you choose it, and you can turn it off (deleting the copy) at any time. We do not sell your information, show ads or track you across other sites.

1. What we collect

Information Why Where it is kept
Your email address and name To create and secure your account, and to send confirmation and password reset emails. Our server database.
A password, if you sign up with email To sign you in. Our server database, only as a salted hash. We cannot read your password.
Google sign-in details, if you use Google To sign you in without a password. See section 4. Our server database.
Sign-in sessions To keep you signed in and to protect your account. A session records when it was created and when it expires, and the IP address and device type it was created from. Our server database. The sign-in token on your phone is kept in the phone's secure storage.
Your fitness and health details: date of birth, sex, height, weight, goals, your plan, workouts, food and calorie logs, water intake, weight and body measurements, InBody records, check-ins and your name and photo in the app To build your plan and targets and to show your history and progress. On your phone, encrypted. If you turn on cloud backup, also on our server, encrypted (see section 2). Otherwise we do not receive them.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Its fonts are served from the site itself.

Reminders you turn on are scheduled on your phone and are not sent to us. A short history of the notifications your phone has shown is kept on your phone for 30 days. The exercise guides are part of the app, so viewing them sends nothing anywhere.

2. Cloud backup (optional)

Cloud backup is off until you turn it on in the app (Settings, then Cloud backup), which you can only do while signed in. When it is on, the app copies your fitness and health details (listed above) to our server, so they are not lost with your phone, you can restore them on a new phone by signing in, and they stay the same across your phones.

  • Your consent. Because these details are about your health, we copy them only because you ask us to, by turning backup on. You can withdraw that at any time by turning it off.
  • Encrypted. The copy is encrypted on our server (AES-256) before it is stored, and each item is tied to your account, so a copy of our database alone does not reveal it.
  • Used only for your backup. We use it only to back up, restore and sync your data for you. We do not read it for any other purpose, analyse it, share it or use it to train anything.
  • Age check. Our server reads the date of birth in your profile to make sure you are 18 or over, and refuses to keep the details of anyone younger.
  • Turning it off deletes the copy on our server straight away. Your details stay on your phone. Deleting your account deletes the copy too.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app: create your account, sign you in and keep it secure, and, if you turn it on, keep your cloud backup.
  • To send the notifications you turned on.
  • To email you about your account (confirming your address, resetting a password). We do not send marketing email.
  • To answer your messages and requests.
  • To protect the service and fix problems (including with crash and error reports).
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you.

4. Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, your email address, whether Google has verified that address, and an identifier for your Google account. Google also provides a link to your profile picture, which we do not display or use. We use this only to create your account and sign you in. We never receive your Google password and we do not access your Google contacts, files or other data.

StokeForm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who handles information for us

We use these providers to run the service. Each handles information only to provide its service to us.

  • Google Cloud hosts our server (in Belgium, europe-west1).
  • Neon hosts our database (including your cloud backup, if you turn it on, encrypted).
  • Resend sends our account emails, so it receives your email address and the message.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you use it.
  • OpenAI reads InBody report images, only if you choose that feature.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to your phone, if you turn on reminders while signed in.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) receives crash and error reports, without personal information.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety or our rights. If we are ever sold or merged, we will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. We choose providers that protect it appropriately.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, it is deleted straight away, with its sessions, notification tokens and cloud backup. If you ask us by email instead, we delete it within 30 days. Copies in routine database backups are removed as the backups expire, within 30 days.
  • Your cloud backup is kept while backup is on. Turning backup off deletes it straight away.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited time and then removed. Crash and error reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Information stored on your phone stays there until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Signing out does not delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

Deleting your account. In the app, go to You, then Settings, then Privacy and account, then Delete account, and confirm. This deletes your account and everything we hold for it on our server straight away (your name, email, sign-in, sessions, linked Google sign-in, notification tokens and cloud backup) and erases the fitness data the app keeps on your phone. If you can't use the app, see how to delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to withdraw your consent, and to object to or limit how it is used. To use any of these rights, email support@stokeform.com from the address on the account so that we can confirm it is you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Your fitness records are on your phone, so you can see, edit and delete them in the app at any time. You can turn cloud backup off in the app at any time.

8. Security

Information sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and the sign-in token on your phone is kept in secure storage. Your fitness records are encrypted (AES-256) in the app's private storage on your phone, with a key kept in the phone's secure, hardware-backed storage that never leaves the phone. Your cloud backup, if you turn it on, is encrypted again on our server. Please protect your phone with a screen lock. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

StokeForm is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18: the app asks for your date of birth, and our server refuses to back up the details of anyone younger. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. If this changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this policy first and tell you in the app before the change applies to you. Cloud backup, added in this version, is off until you turn it on. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: support@stokeform.com.
