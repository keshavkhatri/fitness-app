The product could have 6 major modules
1. Workout Planner
   - Pre-built workout programs
   - Custom workout plans
   - Muscle-group based workouts
   - Exercises with GIF/video demonstrations
   - Sets, reps, weight, rest timer
   - Progressive overload recommendations
   - Weekly/monthly workout schedule
2. Daily Workout Tracking
   - "Today's Workout"
   - Log sets/reps/weight
   - Auto-fill previous weights
   - Personal records
   - Workout history
   - Workout duration
   - Volume tracking
   - Calories burned estimate
   - Exercise performance charts
3. InBody / Body Composition
   - Upload InBody PDF/image
   - Extract:
     - Weight
     - Body-fat %
     - Skeletal muscle mass
     - BMI
     - Visceral fat
     - Segmental muscle/fat
     - BMR
     - etc.
   - Compare reports over time
   - Identify areas that need improvement
   - Generate a recommended training/nutrition strategy
4. Nutrition / Meal Tracking
   - Daily calorie target
   - Protein/carbs/fat targets
   - Food database
   - Search food
   - Scan/photograph food
   - Add custom food
   - Meals and recipes
   - Daily/weekly nutrition history
   - Calories remaining
   - Macro progress
5. Progress & Coaching
   - Weight trend
   - Body-fat trend
   - Muscle gain
   - Workout progression
   - Measurements
   - Progress photos
   - Goal tracking
   - Adaptive recommendations
6. Exercise Library
   - Exercise GIF/video
   - Muscle targeted
   - Equipment required
   - Difficulty
   - Instructions
   - Common mistakes
   - Alternatives
   - Beginner/intermediate/advanced variations
